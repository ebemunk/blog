Making Of: Ten Million Chess Openings

1 January 0001
on: chess, data visualization, long form, making of

Whenever I share something I make, I get a lot of emails and comments, and by far the most common question is: “how did you make this?” The honest answer is usually “slowly, and with a lot of fiddling”, but I figured it deserves a better answer than that. This blog is open-source, the common tools I use are d3, and custom code. But no, I can’t help you with tech support on this repo. Most are disheartened when they hear this; they want to hear there’s some magic software that can just do it (and nowadays there just might be, with the proliferation of AI tools, that’s another post).
